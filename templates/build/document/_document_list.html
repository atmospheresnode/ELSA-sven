{% comment %} The documents in a bundle's document collection as a grid of tiles, for Archive and External alike. Each tile shows the document itself: the top of a PDF's first page, or the opening lines of a text file. Clicking that opens the shared #documentPreviewModal. Under it are the name, the file, and Download, Edit and Delete. Delete opens the shared #documentDeleteModal (_document_delete_modal.html). Both modals must be on the page. The last tile adds a document. PDF thumbnails are drawn in the browser by PDF.js, loaded only when a PDF tile scrolls into view. Nothing runs on the server against uploaded files (see document_files on why not), and a thumbnail that cannot be drawn just keeps its icon. PDFs over 15 MB keep the icon too, rather than being fetched whole for a picture.
