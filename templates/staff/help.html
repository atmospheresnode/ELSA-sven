Getting started with the Admin Console

A five-minute tour. Every page also has "Help for this page" at the top right.

A normal day

  1. Open Today. It lists the requests that need you and the reviews you could pick up.
  2. Open a request. The Next step box at the top shows only what fits it now: reply, take it, approve, ask for changes. Everything else is under More actions.
  3. Reply. The user gets it in ELSA and by email, and can answer either way. Their answer brings the request back to Today.
  4. When it is done, choose Reply and resolve, or just Resolve.

Where things are

Today
What needs you now.
Requests
Everything users have asked: agency changes, submissions, contact forms, storage, messages.
Reviews
The pipeline of submitted bundles, provider projects and outside peer reviews.
People and bundles
Find any user or bundle, and bundles that have gone quiet.
Team
Saved replies, knowledge notes, who has what, announcements and email to users.
Oversight
Reports, validate insights, the email log, the audit log, system health and settings.

The search box at the top (Ctrl+K) jumps to any request number, user or bundle.

Reviewing a bundle

  1. A submission arrives as a request at the stage Submitted. Choose Start review; the owner is told.
  2. Read the files on the bundle's page. Add a lien for each problem; you can point at a file and line.
  3. Choose Ask for changes with a message, or Accept once the checklist is ticked and no liens are open.
  4. An accepted bundle is released step by step from its release page, ending with the DOI going to the owner.

Staying safe

  • Admin mode ends after 30 quiet minutes or 8 hours, and if your session turns up in another browser or network. A warning appears before it ends.
  • Buttons marked passkey ask for a passkey tap: they change an account or let you see what a user sees.
  • Everything you do is in the audit log, sealed so it cannot be quietly changed.
  • You cannot decide your own request or review your own bundle; a colleague does.
